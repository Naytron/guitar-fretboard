About this diagram

Choose a root and this shows the common chord qualities built on it — the major, minor, dominant, diminished, suspended, added, and augmented families — each drawn across the whole neck. Every place a chord tone appears is marked and colored by its interval, so you can see the shape of a chord instead of memorizing a single box.

The notes are calculated from the open-string tuning (note = (open + fret) mod 12) and each tone is labelled with its true interval (so an add9's ninth reads 9 and a diminished 7th reads ♭♭7). Spelling follows the selected key; nothing here is reproduced from copyrighted sheet music.
